A simple case for school uniforms

Less outfit pressure. More room to learn.

School is for trying new things, finding your people, and learning something every day. A shared uniform can make the start of that day a little easier.

Ready for the day

A small change can make the school day feel simpler.

Uniforms don't do the learning for us. They can clear away a few everyday distractions so students can focus on what matters.

More energy for the school day.

Keep the focus on ideas, friendships, and the things students do.

  • Make mornings easier

    One less outfit decision can mean a calmer start and less time wondering what to wear.

  • Turn down clothing pressure

    When classmates share a basic look, brands and trends can matter less in the classroom.

  • Show we're in it together

    A shared uniform is a visible reminder that everyone belongs to the same school community.

Uniforms work best when everyone can wear them.

Comfort matters. So does cost. Schools should offer practical, affordable options that work for every student.
